# To new members or returning members

*Published: 2012-07-10*

Just a reminder to those who are new to the guild as well as those who may be returning. Those at the Tiger Blood or Occifers rank have the final say in dealing with guild matters and should be treated accordingly. They have the final say as to who can stay and who must go in the guild. Treat the with the utmost respect and try not to force them to make decisions which will impact your membership in the guild.
