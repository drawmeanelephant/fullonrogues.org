Founded in August 2007, **Full on Rogues** was Feathermoon's premier end-page reading guild — a crew of rogues, alts, and assorted troublemakers on the Feathermoon RP server who took over the guild nerd-points leaderboard, farmed achievements nobody asked for, and documented all of it with a completely straight face.

The name is a vanilla-era Ironforge joke. Stand near the bank long enough and the trade chat spam would hit you with it: *LFM UBRS — FULL ON ROGUES.* The group wasn't recruiting rogues; it was full on rogues. Five years of running that pun into the ground is what this archive documents.

This site is the archive of that run: every guild-blog post from 2010 to 2012, plus the screenshot vault. Nothing here has been cleaned up, corrected, or explained.

## Reading the archive

Start at the [Guild Blog Archives](posts.md). The posts are numbered in the order they were recovered (`POST-0001` through `POST-0032`) and cover recruitment drama, patch days, achievement pushes, nerd-points standings, and the ongoing Deezz situation.

## Colophon

The original site was a WordPress blog that went dark when the guild did. This archive was rebuilt as a static site with [Boris](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/boris) and served by Cloudflare Pages. The posts are preserved verbatim, typos included, because that is the point.
